How to play

Charged wedge molecules jostle in a heat bath and click together into rings. Each new bond plays a note. Tap the floor to drop a bell; anything that touches a bell rings it. Drag bells to move them. Double-tap a bell to remove it.

Q–U and Z–M play the duet pads. Arrow keys and Space play the sampler. K plays the color under the cursor. L sends a laser ship. P transmogrifies.
